On July 26, 2026, we renewed the [official Acecore Systems website](/en/).

This update was about more than changing colors and layouts. We renewed the entire site to communicate not only what Acecore Systems builds, but also how we understand business operations and how we continue supporting them after launch.

## What we wanted the renewal to communicate more clearly

The previous site already described our commitment to supporting clients from requirements discovery through development and operational improvement. Yet listing development areas and features separately was not enough to express how we connect day-to-day work with the data and permissions underneath it and with the maintenance required after launch.

For this renewal, we did more than rearrange development areas and features. We rebuilt the copy, information architecture, visual language, and consultation journey to present Acecore Systems as a service that **designs the workplace, systems, and operations of a business as one whole**.

The new structure first communicates that a conversation can begin not only with "What can you build?" but with "What would let this work continue smoothly and sustainably?"

## The theme is "Quiet Infrastructure"

The new theme is "QUIET INFRASTRUCTURE."

For a business system, standing out matters less than being easy to use in everyday work, resilient when exceptions occur, and adaptable as circumstances change. It supports operations reliably even when people rarely notice it. The phrase "Quiet Infrastructure" captures that role.

Visually, the site combines a bright, paper-like surface with structures underneath that evoke stone and piping, accented by navy and blue. We use a Mincho-style serif face for large messages and a Gothic-style sans serif for navigation and explanations, separating the parts that convey our philosophy from the parts people operate.

The three-layer cross-section below the hero is not merely decorative. It visualizes how the workplace people interact with, the systems that organize data and permissions, and the operations that continue monitoring and improvement overlap to keep one business process moving.

## Viewing work through three layers: workplace, systems, and operations

The entire site is organized around these three shared layers:

- **Usable in the workplace:** Organize users, the order of decisions, and the flow of input and review so everyday work can progress naturally
- **Supported by systems:** Connect data, permissions, external integrations, and exception handling to support secure, consistent processing
- **Improved through operations:** Plan for monitoring, maintenance, inquiries, and improvement so the system can continue adapting after launch

Building only the interface does not solve the problem if the data and permissions beneath it remain disorganized; it merely moves the burden elsewhere. Conversely, even a technically correct system will not take root if its interface leaves users confused.

We consider the three layers separately, then connect them into one operation. This is the idea the renewal makes most explicit.

## A structure that leads from a challenge to the right information

The new website is designed not only for visitors who already know which feature they want, but also for those who have not yet found the words for their challenge.

The [Services page](/en/services/) introduces support for business systems, web applications and admin interfaces, data and third-party service integration, and improvements to existing systems. The [Implementation Guide](/en/guide/) explains the path from initial consultation through requirements discovery, design, development, launch, and operational improvement.

The [Work page](/en/works/) presents projects we can share publicly, following the sequence from challenge to proposal and outcome. [Pricing guidance](/en/pricing/) and [Contact](/en/contact/) are also available as separate entry points so visitors can proceed according to their stage of consideration.

The home page also offers entry points for situations such as "copying and manual work keep increasing," "the current system does not fit how people actually work," and "data and tools are fragmented." Even without knowing the name of a feature, visitors can move from the problem they are experiencing to the next relevant information.

## Clarifying the connection with the main Acecore website

We also reworked how the main Acecore website and Acecore Systems connect.

The [Acecore services directory](https://acecore.net/en/services/) is the general point of contact for exploring system development, server operations, web production, design, education, and other areas. Detailed information about business systems, web applications, admin interfaces, and data integration now leads to Acecore Systems, where visitors can review specialized support and pricing.

Visitors can also move from the [Acecore pricing page](https://acecore.net/en/pricing/) to Systems development pricing. When they decide to contact us, the [service CTA carries its context into the contact form](/en/insights/service-cta-contact-prefill/), reducing the need to select the subject of their consultation again.

Rather than presenting the parent brand and specialist service as separate destinations, we designed a relationship in which visitors can move naturally between them according to what they need.

## Release checks that go beyond appearance

The site continues to use its existing Astro and Cloudflare Pages foundation. Published information is managed as JSON data and can also be updated through Sveltia CMS. The thinking behind the site's features and publishing foundation is covered in [the overall architecture for extending an official website with Astro and Cloudflare](/en/insights/astro-cloudflare-site-architecture/).

For this renewal, we checked desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts for unnatural line breaks and horizontal overflow. We also inspected internal and in-page links on key pages, the journey from pricing to contact, and the mobile menu before release.

The [implementation details and design QA for the release are available in GitHub Pull Request #14](https://github.com/acecore-systems/acecore-systems/pull/14).

We continue to expand the site after launch. It now includes more detailed information about IT advisory services, AI adoption, deployment support, and adding CMS, AI, search, and form capabilities to existing websites. We treat the renewal not as a finish line, but as a foundation for making our services clearer over time.

## Summary

For the Acecore Systems website renewal, we translated the idea of viewing work through the three layers of workplace, systems, and operations into the copy, information architecture, visual language, and consultation journey under the theme of "Quiet Infrastructure."

You do not need to have decided what to build. We can begin by organizing situations such as excessive copying, a system that is difficult to use, or tools that do not connect with one another.

Start by exploring our [services](/en/services/), or [contact us about a business system or web application](/en/contact/?category=service&service=business-system&from=systems-insight&entry=acecore-systems-site-renewal#contact-form).
